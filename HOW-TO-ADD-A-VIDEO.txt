MANE STUDIOS site: how to add a video
=====================================

Everything you watch on the site is listed in one file:  js/catalog.js
You never need to touch the other files.

1. Put the video online
   Upload the .mp4 to your video storage (Cloudflare R2) and copy its link.
   It looks like:  https://your-bucket-link/tosl-s2e2.mp4
   (YouTube also works. Write it as  youtube:VIDEO_ID  , where VIDEO_ID is
   the part after "watch?v=" in the YouTube link.)

2. Paste the link into catalog.js
   Find the episode or movie and put the link between the quotes after video:
     { title: "Handoff", video: "" }
   becomes
     { title: "Handoff", length: "3:00", video: "https://your-bucket-link/tosl-s2e2.mp4" }
   As soon as a title has a link, "Coming soon" turns into a Play button.

3. Add a new episode
   Copy an episode line, paste it under the last one, and change the title,
   the about line and the link. Keep the comma between lines.

4. Add a new show or movie
   Copy a whole title block (for example "blood-money": { ... }), give it a
   new short id, change the words, then add that id to a row at the top
   under rows: so it shows up on the home page.

5. Posters
   Titles without a picture get a color poster with the name on it.
   To use a real poster, put the image in the media folder and add
     poster: "media/your-poster.jpg"     (tall, 2:3)
     backdrop: "media/your-banner.jpg"   (wide, 16:9)

6. Ratings
   "TV-MA" titles ask viewers to confirm they're 18 or older before playing.

Note: The Other Side of Love, Season 2, Episode 1 currently plays a 10-second
placeholder (media/sample-clip.mp4). Swap in the real episode link when it's
uploaded, then delete that sample file.
